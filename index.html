06 · Research

Research 科研经历

2025

Integrated Photovoltaic & Fish Aquaculture Systems

Pioneer Research · Pioneer Research 夏校

Conducted independent research under the mentorship of Duke University Professor Zbigniew Kabala. Compared the hydrodynamic responses of rectangular, octagonal, and hexadecagonal cage structures in an integrated photovoltaic and fish-aquaculture system, using fluid-mechanics modeling to evaluate cage design and fish-welfare implications.
在杜克大学 Zbigniew Kabala 教授指导下开展独立研究，比较光伏渔业一体化系统中矩形、八边形与十六边形网箱的水动力响应，并通过流体力学建模评估网箱结构设计及其对鱼类福利的影响。
